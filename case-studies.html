AnnouncePro / Desktop App

Challenge: Schools, universities, and corporate offices relied on manual PA systems or informal group chats to broadcast announcements — resulting in missed messages, zero scheduling control, no audio management, and no activity logs.

Solution: Velumo built AnnouncePro — a fully offline Windows desktop application with smart date/time scheduling, MP3 & WAV playback, daily repeat modes, system volume control, real-time monitoring logs, and a polished modern UI. Released as free, open-source software under the MIT license with zero telemetry or data collection.

CampusReveal / Web App

Challenge: Students had no centralized, visually engaging platform to discover what's happening on their campus. Events, clubs, departments, and opportunities were scattered across physical notice boards, WhatsApp groups, and disconnected faculty pages — creating information gaps and low student engagement.

Solution: Velumo designed and deployed CampusReveal — a React single-page web application offering an elegant, card-based discovery experience for campus life. The app enables students to explore and engage with campus events and communities through a fast, modern interface served globally via Vercel's CDN edge network.
