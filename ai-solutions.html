FAQ

How much does an AI solution cost?+

Pricing depends on complexity and integrations. Most projects begin with a scoped pilot before full rollout.

How long does implementation take?+

Simple use-cases can launch in 2-4 weeks. Multi-system deployments typically take 6-10 weeks.

Can AI work with our current tools?+

Yes. We design around your existing CRM, support tools, and databases where possible.

Do we need clean data first?+

Not always. We usually include data normalization in early implementation phases.

Will this replace our team?+

No. Our focus is augmentation: freeing your team from repetitive work and improving decision quality.

Do you provide support after launch?+

Yes, with monitoring, retraining support, and iterative enhancement plans.
